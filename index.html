Pest Information & Treatment Details

Rodent Management

Rodents (rats, mice, and bandicoots) are destructive urban pests that contaminate food, spread diseases, and cause severe structural damage. Their constant gnawing on wires and cables is a leading cause of electrical short circuits, fires, and communication network collapses.

Our service provides total and effective rodent management using a new-age combination of strategic baiting, trapping, and proofing methods.

Features & Benefits

Baits are placed in strategic locations where rodents frequently visit.
Use of glue boards/traps is integrated into operations and carried out wherever needed.
Uses registered products targeted at total management.
Our experts suggest sanitation and proofing measures from time to time to prevent rodent entry.

Common Target Rodent Species

House Mouse

Mus musculus

A small species, dusty grey-brown with a lighter belly. Extremely active, nest indoors in walls and cabinets, and feed on diverse foods.

House Rat (Roof Rat)

Rattus rattus

A large species, sleek, dark brown/black with a tail longer than its head and body. Excellent climbers nesting in roofs, attics, and tall structures.

Professional Grade Management Equipment

Tamper-Resistant Bait Stations

Secure, lockable stations designed to safely house rodent bait, keeping it protected from children, pets, and non-target animals.

Industrial Strength Glue Traps

Non-toxic, flat-folding adhesive boards placed along walls and active runways for immediate, pesticide-free capturing.

Termite Management

Termites (often called white ants) are silent destroyers organized in massive subterranean colonies of millions. A single mature queen can produce up to 10,000 eggs per week (30 to 60 million eggs in her lifetime), making them a rapid and relentless threat.

They feed on cellulose materials (doors, furniture, documents, clothes, currency notes) and also damage non-cellulose items like wire coatings, rubber, and thermocol. As hidden invaders, they often cause heavy structural damage before they are discovered.

Post-Construction Treatment Method

We carry out treatment in the junction of floor & wall by drilling holes, injecting insecticide to soak the area, and sealing them. This creates a completely undetectable chemical barrier around your property.

Benefits of Termite Treatment

Kills termites (doesn't just repel them)
Next-generation chemicals used worldwide
Safe for your family's health
Zero inconvenience during service

Cockroach Management

Cockroaches are highly resilient, adaptable pests found globally in homes, offices, hotels, and restaurants. Capable of surviving extreme conditions, they are key vectors that contaminate food, spread diseases, and trigger allergies in humans.

They chew on fabric, bookbindings, and papers, causing property damage. Our clean gel treatment targets their hideouts directly, providing total management of cockroaches and other crawling pests like ants, silverfish, and spiders without any disruption.

Benefits of Gel Treatment

Safer for health, property & environment
Eco-friendly & non-polluting
Odourless & non-messy (no smell)
No need to vacate during/after treatment
Next-generation global treatments
Highly effective & long-lasting

Common Target Cockroach Species

American Cockroach

Periplaneta americana

A large species, typically reddish-brown and found in warm, damp environments such as sewers, basements, and drains. Highly active flyers.

German Cockroach

Blattella germanica

A rapid breeding small, light brown species with two dark stripes on the back. Infests indoor kitchens, pantries, and food-prep areas.

Mosquito Management
